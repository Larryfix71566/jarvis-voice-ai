Approved by Larry, 2026-09-30: A2 transcript rows (chosen over bubbles so long replies read at full width), B1 compact cards, the Recents menu as shown (view buttons move into it), D2 reference line for reuse, and the new-result notice instead of a jump. CC7a.1–CC7a.4 are built to this design; Codex reviews each PR against it and plan §7.2.
Already decided (you, 09-30, and Codex's CX-15 boundaries). These hold in every option:
  • One conversation thread with both sides, full text. Today the stage shows only the last two turns, cut at 160 characters (latestCaptions, liveCaption).
  • Spoken answers stop creating a result each. Structured results (weather, research, images) become cards in the thread.
  • A Recents menu replaces the tab strip, showing pinned results first, then about 10 recent ones. That's only a display limit: the app still keeps up to 20 unpinned results, and nothing open or pinned is removed.
  • Asking again reuses a result: fresh weather (under 15 minutes) reopens; older weather refreshes under the same identity. There are never duplicates.
  • A new card never pulls you away from what you're reading. Today weather jumps to the front (AppMessageRouter calls select); CC7a changes that.
  • Voice keeps working by result ID: "open number 2" or "open the Folly Beach weather" is matched against Recents. If two results match, or the list has changed, Mortimer asks you instead of guessing.
